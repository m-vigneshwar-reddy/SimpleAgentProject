# 📍 UPDATE: Detailed Place Information Feature

## 🎉 What's New?

Your travel planner now includes **comprehensive place information** for every activity in your itinerary!

### ✨ New Information for Each Place

Every activity now includes:

1. **📍 Place Name** - Specific attraction, restaurant, or venue name
2. **🏛️ Activity Type** - What you'll be doing there
3. **📮 Address/Area** - Where to find it
4. **🕒 Opening Hours** - When it's open
5. **💰 Entrance Fee** - Cost to visit (if any)
6. **⏱️ Duration** - How long to spend there
7. **⭐ Rating** - Quality rating (1-5 stars)
8. **🎯 Best Time to Visit** - Optimal visiting time
9. **📋 Category** - Type of activity (Culture, Food, Nature, etc.)
10. **💡 Tips & Recommendations** - Insider advice
11. **📅 Booking Required?** - Whether advance booking is needed

## 💡 Tips for Using This Feature

### 1. **Check Opening Hours**
Always verify opening hours, especially for:
- Museums (often closed Mondays)
- Religious sites (limited access during services)
- Restaurants (may have afternoon closures)

### 2. **Book in Advance**
When you see "📅 Booking Recommended":
- Make reservations ASAP
- Check cancellation policies
- Save confirmation emails

### 3. **Use Addresses for Planning**
- Add addresses to Google Maps
- Calculate actual travel times
- Plan walking routes

### 4. **Read the Tips**
The tips section includes:
- Money-saving advice
- Crowd avoidance strategies
- What to bring/wear
- Local customs

### 5. **Best Time to Visit**
Follow the "Best time" suggestions:
- Avoid peak crowds
- Get better photos
- Enjoy better weather
